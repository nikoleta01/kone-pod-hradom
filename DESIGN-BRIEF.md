# Design brief — Kone pod hradom

Source: client e-mail from Timea Szárková, 31 July 2026.
This is the raw material for `/impeccable`. Keep it factual — no invented claims.

## Client

**OZ Tymián – kone pod hradom**, a civic association in **Krásnohorské
Podhradie** (Rožňava district, below Krásna Hôrka Castle).

Their work: experiences with horses, nature and environmental education.

## What visitors can do

- Horse riding, and led rides for children
- Meeting the horses and helping care for them on the grounds
- Events and themed workshops throughout the year
- Programmes at the upcoming Eco-centre by the horses

## The project being funded

**Ekovan – Ekocentrum pri koňoch.** A space joining contact with horses,
environmental education and rest in nature. It will contain:

- an educational space in a shepherd hut (maringotka)
- a zone for creative and educational activities
- a herb garden
- a sensory trail
- a riding arena
- visitor facilities

Goal: a place for families with children, schools and travellers to have
authentic contact with nature and animals.

## Audience

1. **Families with young children** — the biggest group. Parents deciding on a
   weekend outing; they need to see it is safe, gentle and suitable for small
   children. Mostly on phones.
2. **Schools and organised groups** — teachers planning a trip. They need
   capacity, duration and how to arrange a date.
3. **Travellers** visiting Krásna Hôrka Castle — looking for something nearby.

## Constraints

- **Mode: Persuade.** The site's job is to make someone decide to visit.
- **Three languages:** Slovak (primary), Hungarian, English. Hungarian matters —
  this is a bilingual region. Text expands in Hungarian; layouts must not break.
- **Mandatory publicity block** for the Terra Incognita programme in the footer:
  three logos plus a funding sentence. It is a funding condition, not decoration
  — it must be present and legible, without dominating the page.
- **Photographs are not available yet.** The design has to hold up with
  placeholders, and be ready to let real horse photography lead once it arrives.
  Assume the client's photos will be phone snapshots, not a studio shoot.
- **Mobile-first.** Weak rural signal — keep it light.
- Reservation system is planned but not built; `/rezervacia` is a placeholder.

## Reference points supplied by the client

Similar Terra Incognita projects, given as inspiration (not as a standard to
copy — both are fairly plain):

- https://hobby-jazda-lipovnik.sk/sk — another riding operation nearby
- https://apiatti.com/ — bee farm in Betliar

## Tone

Warm, grounded, unpretentious. A small family operation, not a resort.
Environmental education is genuinely part of the offer, not a marketing angle.

## Current state

The scaffold ships a deliberately quiet placeholder palette (warm cream, moss
green, clay) in `src/app/globals.css`. It is a starting point, not a decision —
it exists so the structure could be judged before the visual direction is set.
