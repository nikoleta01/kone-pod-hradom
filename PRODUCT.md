## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Families with young children** — the largest group and the design's centre
   of gravity. A parent deciding on a weekend outing, usually on a phone, rural areas. Their job: establish quickly that this is safe, gentle
   and genuinely suitable for a small child, then act.
2. **Teachers and organised groups** — planning a school or kindergarten trip.
   Their job: find capacity, duration and how to arrange a date, then get a
   human on the phone.
3. **Travellers at Krásna Hôrka Castle** — already in the area, looking for
   something nearby worth an extra hour or two.

## Product Purpose

A public site for **OZ Tymián – kone pod hradom**, a civic association in
Krásnohorské Podhradie (Rožňava district), below Krásna Hôrka Castle. The
association runs experiences with horses alongside nature and environmental
education.

Success is a visit: the site's job is to move someone from "maybe" to picking
up the phone. It is not a booking system and not a brochure.

## Positioning

Contact with horses and environmental education are the same offer here, not a
horse operation with an eco-theme bolted on. The **Ekovan – Ekocentrum pri
koňoch** project makes that literal: a shepherd hut (maringotka) as a teaching
space, a herb garden, a sensory trail and a riding arena on one site.

Small, family-run, below a landmark castle. Not a resort, not a riding school
selling lesson packages.

## Operating Context

- Rural site below Krásna Hôrka Castle; visitors arrive by car, frequently as a
  side trip from the castle.
- Weak mobile signal on and around the site. Pages must be light.
- The association's live presence is on Facebook and Instagram; the site must
  work alongside those, not compete with them.
- Terra Incognita is the funding programme; its publicity requirements are a
  condition of the grant.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **OZ Tymián – kone pod hradom**.
- Tone: warm, grounded, unpretentious. A small family operation. Environmental
  education is genuinely part of the offer and must not be written as a
  marketing angle.
- Client-supplied reference points, offered as context and explicitly _not_ as
  a standard to match: <https://hobby-jazda-lipovnik.sk/sk> and
  <https://apiatti.com/>.
- No association logo or wordmark exists. Any mark is to be designed, and must
  be identified as newly made rather than presented as existing identity.
- **Incumbent palette, from the association's own published posters: deep
  olive/forest green, parchment cream, and wood brown.** Green carries their
  badges, headings and icon discs; cream is the ground; brown is structural
  (wooden sign bars, twine). Treat the _hues_ as a brand commitment.
  Deliberately NOT inherited: the poster's execution — distressed textures,
  torn-paper edges, scattered pinecone and mushroom ornament, circular photo
  vignettes, and stock or AI-generated imagery. That is a template look, and
  reproducing it would land the site in exactly the generic register the
  redesign exists to escape. Honour the colours and the warmth; give them a
  structure the posters do not have.
- Their voice in Slovak is informal and addresses one child directly
  ("Tešíme sa na teba"), not a formal plural.

## Evidence on Hand

**Confirmed by the client's own published material.** Recovered from an event
poster the association published to social media. These are real and may be
stated — but note the scope limit.

- **Tvorivé dielne** (creative workshops) using natural materials, run **in the
  spirit of _lesná pedagogika_ — forest pedagogy**. This is the association's
  own term for its method and outranks the generic phrase "environmental
  education" in their voice.
- The workshop runs **Tuesdays 14:00–15:00**, **for children aged 5 to 10**.
  **Scope limit:** these terms belong to that workshop specifically. They are
  NOT confirmed as the age range or schedule for riding, led rides, or visits
  in general, and must not be presented as site-wide facts.
- The workshop's four stated parts: creative work from natural materials;
  forest pedagogy ("objavujeme les všetkými zmyslami" — discovering the forest
  with every sense); **"pokojné a bezpečné vozenie sa na koni"** — calm and
  safe riding in nature; and experiences that build creativity, a relationship
  with nature, and self-confidence.
- Their own framing of the horses: **"Kone — naši kamaráti a učitelia"**
  (horses, our friends and teachers). This is the association's thesis stated
  in their own words and should lead the site's argument.
- Closing voice: informal second-person singular — "Tešíme sa na teba."

## Product Principles

1. **A parent's doubt is the real obstacle.** Safety, gentleness and
   suitability for a small child are the argument; everything else is support.
2. **The phone number is the product's only conversion.** It is never more than
   a thumb's reach away, and it never hides behind a form.
3. **Never fabricate proof.** Where a real fact is missing, the design holds a
   visible slot for it rather than inventing a substitute or quietly dropping
   the claim.
4. **Hungarian is a first-class layout case**, tested at the same moment as
   Slovak — not a translation applied to a Slovak-shaped layout.
5. **Weight is a design constraint.** Weak rural signal means every kilobyte is
   a decision, and the site must be usable before it is fully loaded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Trilingual SK/HU/EN with correct `lang` handling; Hungarian text expansion
  must not break any layout.
- Phone-first: touch targets, tappable `tel:` link, legible outdoors on a
  mid-range Android in daylight.
- Terra Incognita publicity block must remain legible at its prescribed minimum
  logo size.
- Keyboard focus is already visible in `globals.css` and stays that way.
